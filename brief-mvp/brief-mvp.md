# Product Brief: Seller Strategy Studio MVP

## Problem Statement
Sellers are overwhelmed by transactional pipeline management, losing sight of the relational and strategic depth required for complex sales. Current tools optimize for volume, not for the quality of the seller's engagement or their own professional evolution.

## Target Outcomes
- **Relational Transformation**: Shift from transactional task-checking to meaningful stakeholder engagement.
- **Strategic Clarity**: Provide sellers with a "mirror" to identify blind spots in their GTM approach.
- **Operational Efficiency**: Automate the "noise" (enrichment, tracking) to free up time for high-value sensing and prototyping.

## User Segments
- **Account Executives (AEs)**: Primary users needing to balance pipeline velocity with strategic deal quality.
- **Sales Leaders**: Managers needing visibility into both pipeline health and the strategic maturity of their team.

## Scope Boundaries
- **In-Scope**: Theory U-inspired sensing (deal sentiment), GTM execution engine (Apollo/Clay/LinkedIn integration), and AI-driven coaching advisor.
- **Out-of-Scope**: Full CRM replacement, complex billing/invoicing, or multi-team enterprise hierarchy management.

## Core Pillars
1. **Reflective Pipeline**: Sensing, Presencing (Case Clinics), and Prototyping.
2. **GTM Execution Engine**: Automated enrichment and intelligent pipeline management.
3. **Strategic Coaching Layer**: AI Advisor for blind-spot identification.
