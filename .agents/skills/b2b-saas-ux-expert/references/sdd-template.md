# SDD template (write in the user's language)

```markdown
# Auditoría UX/UI de <app> — mejoras propuestas

## Resumen
- 3–5 sentences: overall verdict, the biggest risks, and what gets fixed first.
- Scope checked: flows, roles, desktop and mobile, accessibility, analytics (yes/no).

## Trabajos del producto
| Trabajo | Quién | Frecuencia | Estado hoy |

## Hallazgos
### P0-1 <short problem title>
- **Problema:** what happens to the user, on which screen. Evidence: screenshot path.
- **A quién afecta:** roles · how often.
- **Referencia:** pattern and product (e.g. "Clay: cost before running").
- **Propuesta:** the concrete change, within the project's design system.
- **Criterio de aceptación:** a verifiable statement ("A rep creates a deal in ≤3 steps from the account page").
- **Esfuerzo:** S / M / L · **Riesgo:** none | changes a rule → needs a decision.

(repeat for P1 and P2)

## Hoja de ruta
- Phase 1 (P0) · Phase 2 (P1) · Phase 3 (P2), each with its items.

## Decisiones para ti
- Items that change business rules, permissions, prices or data.

## No comprobado
- Flows, roles or devices that could not be tested, and why.
```
